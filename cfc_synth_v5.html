CFC/AM: modulator shapes carrier amplitude — high κ + depth=1 + sync = pulse train / harmonic buzz.
FM: modulator shifts carrier frequency — index controls sideband density. Harmonic ratios → tonal. Non-integer → clangorous/metallic.
PM: modulator shifts carrier phase — similar to FM but softer fundamental, brighter at high index. Yamaha DX7 is PM, not FM.
Ring Mod: straight multiplication — sum+difference only, no carrier fundamental. Carrier disappears, sidebands only.
PWM: modulates duty cycle of square wave — creates hollow phasing chorus sound at audio-rate modulation.
CFC+FM: envelope gates amplitude AND FM pitches the carrier simultaneously — very thick at high index + sync.
