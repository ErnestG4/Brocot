# The Map Field

Two layers over the ratio axis, both in closed form, both derived from the
coincidence horizon. This is the display spec for a **per-pair** overlay on
brocot's map.

Generated by `criticality_tool/cross_substrate/brocot_two_layer_field.py`, which
asserts every claim below and fails its run if any is false. Field data:
`brocot_two_layer_field.json`.

> ## Scope — load-bearing, not a footnote
>
> **Two operators.** The coincidence rate among above-horizon parameter sets runs
> 4.7% / 17.3% / 44.5% / 65.9% at N = 2…5 and crosses 0.9 by N = 7 (validated
> estimator, `brocot_dissolution_curve`). **At the shipped map's N — up to 16 —
> essentially every parameter set coincides somewhere, and there is nothing to
> categorise.** The field is vacuous there. It must not be built into a
> patch-level view.
>
> **Direct (unfolded) channel, two sine modulators, ε = 1e-3** — the horizon's
> own scope, inherited unchanged. See `COINCIDENCE-HORIZON.md`.
>
> **α ∈ (1/A, A).** Outside that the parent degenerates: no in-box ringing ratio
> exists and the nearest lattice point is the carrier itself. brocot's [0.70,
> 1.40] sits deep inside (1/8, 8), but the display needs the degenerate category
> if the ratio range is ever widened.

## The instrument already had the decomposition

`source/audio/Operator.h`:

> *The SB path picks **which** rational; detune sets **how far off** it the
> effective ratio sits.*

A ratio is stored as **base Stern-Brocot rational × detune in cents**. That is
the same decomposition this document derives from the mathematics — nearest
in-box rational, plus a displacement from it — arrived at years earlier, from
the ear, for unrelated reasons.

This is the third time in this arc that the instrument turns out to have been
built the way the mathematics later says the space is organised: the spec's
curated ratios formed a depth-ordered ladder before anyone knew there was a
depth ordering, the near-unity regime turned out to be the habitable zone rather
than a stylistic preference, and now the parameter decomposition turns out to be
the perceptual one. **Design by ear; explanation arriving late.** The field
below is not a new coordinate imposed on the instrument — it is a readout of the
one the instrument already uses.

The one place the analogy breaks is worth stating, because it costs something:
the perceptual parent is **not** the operator's stored path prefix. Measured at
30.1%. The map needs the argmin, not the string. See *Not the nearest ancestor*.

## The two layers

| layer | what it is | shape | drawn as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **REGION** | the nearest ringing ratio — the in-box rational the spectrum sits closest to sharing a sideband with | piecewise constant in α | a fill |
| **EVENT** | where partials merge — a spectral discontinuity | isolated points | a tick |

They do not collide, and that is a theorem rather than a layout choice.

## Why a ratio has a parent at all

Below the horizon — `max(p, q) ≤ 2·order_bound(I)` — two modulators at 1 and p/q
share a sideband exactly, and the spectrum fuses. Above it nothing fuses, and
every gross descriptor reads identically: at I = 0.9 every node with q ≥ 7
returns 31 partials, top-5 energy 0.716, spectral entropy 0.709.

What distinguishes them is the *closest approach* to a coincidence:

```
gap(a) = min over nonzero |a1|,|a2| <= A  of  |a1 + a2*alpha|     A = 2*order_bound(I)
```

Exactly 0 below the horizon. Above it, strictly positive — and physically a
**frequency difference**: two partials separated by gap·f_c. The pair (a₁, a₂)
attaining the minimum names a rational `|a₁|/|a₂|`, the **parent**.

### What is proved, and what is measured

Both of the 100%s this result was first reported with have been resolved, and
they resolve differently.

| claim | status |
|---|---|
| the parent lies below the horizon | **definitional** — the box forces `max(p,q) ≤ A`. Never evidence; the constraint restated. Kept as a wiring check. |
| the parent is a Stern-Brocot ancestor | **theorem on α ∈ (1/A, A)** — in fact a *convergent*. Confirmed on 37 023 ratios across three populations and three horizons. |
| ties select a convergent too | **empirical, n = 304** — Lagrange needs a strict minimum, so the tie case is unproved. It never failed. |
| α outside (1/A, A) | **boundary, characterised** — the parent degenerates to 0: no in-box ringing ratio, nearest lattice point is the carrier. |

*Proof sketch.* Take a₂ > 0 and write q = a₂, p = −a₁, so the objective is
|qα − p|. The usable q form a **prefix** 1…Q′: qα increases with q, so round(qα)
is non-decreasing, and any q whose best p exceeds A is beaten by q = 1. The
minimiser over a prefix beats every smaller denominator, so it is a best
approximation of the second kind — and by Lagrange, a convergent of α. Every
convergent lies on the Stern-Brocot descent to α. ∎

So `332/332` was confirmation of a proof, not evidence for a rate. The
distinction matters because it removes the boundary case someone would otherwise
probe: the theorem says exactly where it stops, and what happens there.

### Not the nearest ancestor

The parent minimises **denominator-weighted** error, `|a₂|·|α − a₁/a₂|`, not
`|α − a₁/a₂|`. A deeper ancestor sits closer in ratio but carries a larger
denominator, and the denominator multiplies the error — the gap is a distance
between real partials, and a partial at n₂ = 7 is not the same partial as one at
n₂ = 1.

```
alpha = 19/18,  A = 8
    deepest in-box ancestor  8/7   |7a - 8| = 11/18 = 0.611   ->  134.4 Hz apart
    nearest ringing ratio    1/1   |a  - 1| =  1/18 = 0.056   ->   12.2 Hz apart
```

**Implementation consequence.** The parent is not a prefix of the stored path —
30.1% — so the map cannot read it off `PullIndex`. It needs the argmin over the
box: (2A+1)² = 289 integer operations at I = 0.9, computed offline with the
other map fields. Cheap, but not the elegant version, and the difference is
worth stating. The parent *is* always an ancestor, so the path bounds the search.

## Why the layers cannot collide

A below-horizon node is its own parent with gap exactly 0, so a whole
neighbourhood of it takes that node as parent. The argmin can therefore only
switch **strictly between** two nodes, and the boundaries land on their mediants
— 8/9 between 7/8 and 1/1, 9/8 between 1/1 and 8/7, 9/7 between 5/4 and 4/3.

Events sit at region *centres*. Boundaries sit where nothing happens.

This was arrived at the wrong way round. The hypothesis under test was that the
audible jumps in a morph *are* the category changes; it was falsified at 0.0%
coverage against 30.4% for the node set, with the eight largest jumps 1.5 grid
steps from a node and 89–140 steps from any boundary. Nothing merges at a
mediant. The negative is what gives the display its cleanest property.

### The field is a Voronoi tessellation

Each region contains **exactly one** event, and that event is the region's own
parent. Asserted at every I, and not designed for. Every fill is one tick's
basin, never a stripe between two.

## The depth control subdivides it

A = 2·order_bound(I), so raising the modulation index moves the horizon out and
splits the field finer:

| I | B | A | regions | events |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.9 | 4 | 8 | 13 | 13 |
| 1.5 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 20 |
| 2.0 | 6 | 12 | 28 | 28 |
| 3.0 | 7 | 14 | 38 | 38 |

Mean truncation depth rises 1.45× from I = 0.9 to I = 3.0. Depth is the field's
second axis, not a decoration on it.

## What the display may say

Gated the same way `COINCIDENCE-HORIZON.md` gates its morph wording, and
**tightened** from this document's first draft.

**May say:** *"nearest ringing ratio: 4/3"*, *"5.5 Hz apart"*, *"fuses"* at a
node. These are structural: the parent is a theorem and the separation is a
frequency difference between predicted partials.

**May not say, yet:** *"heard as a detuned 4/3."* That is a perceptual claim
carried by structural evidence plus one statistic (79.2% of above-horizon ratios
separate by under 20 Hz). It is probably right and it is not yet earned. The
separation crosses perceptual regimes *within a single region* — in the 4/3
basin, 53/40 sits 5.5 Hz away and 9/7 sits 31.4 Hz away, which are
fusion-with-beating and near-separate-tones. A label reading "detuned 4/3" for
both claims a sameness the numbers do not support.

If the UI wants perceptual language, the honest form is **regime bands on the
separation**, not on the region:

| separation @ f_c | band | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | fuses | partials coincide exactly |
| < 8 Hz | slow beat | one tone, wavering |
| 8 – 20 Hz | rough | one tone, buzzing |
| > 20 Hz | separate | two audible components |

**Gating:** *"heard as"* waits on the ERB check or on listening
(`queue.py: heard-as-listening`). Cheap to add later; expensive to walk back
from a shipped label.

**May not say** that regions are perceptually equal-width — they are not, and
four linearisation attempts failed on two coordinates (`brocot_linear_morph`;
`brocot_linear_morph_erb`, reduction 0.985× against a 3× bar). Nor that a region
boundary is audible; it is specifically where nothing happens.

## What this changes about the instrument

The field is a readout, but three of its properties are design constraints, and
they point the same way: **organise around basins, not around the ratio axis.**

1. **The ratio control should snap to basins, not to nodes.** A player dragging a
   ratio crosses 13 regions with no feedback about which one they are in. The
   basin is the perceptually stable unit — everything inside it is the same
   ringing ratio at a different separation — so detents belong at the
   *boundaries*, where the category changes and nothing else does, and the
   within-basin sweep should be continuous and fine.

2. **Detune should read in Hz, not only cents, above the horizon.** Cents are the
   right unit for the parameter; the separation is the right unit for what is
   heard, and it is not proportional to cents — it scales with the carrier and
   with the parent's denominator. The same 10-cent detune is a slow waver on 1/1
   and a buzz on 7/5. A dual readout costs nothing and the Hz side is the one
   that predicts the sound.

3. **The depth control is a resolution control and should look like one.**
   A = 2·order_bound(I) means raising I subdivides the ratio space 13 → 38.
   Depth is currently presented as brightness or drive; it is *also* the zoom
   level of the ratio field, and the map should redraw as it moves.

4. **The carrier category needs to exist.** Outside (1/A, A) there is no in-box
   ringing ratio at all. If the ratio range is ever widened past 8:1, the field
   needs a state for "nothing to be near" rather than reporting a nearest ratio
   it does not have.

None of this touches the audio thread. The field is offline, the argmin is 289
integer operations, and the redraw is a map-side concern.

## Open

The node set accounts for only **30.4%** of large timbral jumps on a morph path.
The remaining ~70% are amplitude-threshold crossings, where a partial crosses
the −50 dB floor at ratios no Farey enumeration knows about. A complete EVENT
layer needs an amplitude scan the horizon cannot supply. Tracked as
`queue.py: amplitude-event-layer`; the shipped display is scoped to structural
events, which is the right boundary until that lands.
