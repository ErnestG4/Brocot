Architecture. Two carriers (f₁, f₂) at a chosen Stern-Brocot interval. Six universal modulators, each with rate-base (sliding from sub-audio to audio), lock-target (free, f₁-sync, f₂-sync, or cascade-lock to another modulator), Stern-Brocot path for the lock ratio, envelope shape with κ, depth, PSK toggle, and target routing. Border colors: amber-left = audio-rate modulator (timbral/spectral role), green-left = sub-audio (envelope/rhythmic role). Rate-locking lets you commit a relationship and have it track when carriers move. Lock-graph topology below the bank shows the directed graph of rate dependencies.
